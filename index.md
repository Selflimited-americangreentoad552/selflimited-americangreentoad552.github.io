## 🩺 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### "Windows protected your PC" Warning
This often appears for newly published software. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway."

### Installation Stuck or Slow
- Close other large programs running in the background
- Make sure you have enough hard drive space (at least 500 MB)
- Try running the installer as Administrator (right-click the file, then "Run as administrator")

### Can't Sign In
- Check your internet connection
- Make sure your email address is spelled correctly
- Try resetting your password using the "Forgot password" link

### Files Not Syncing Between Devices
- Confirm all devices are connected to the internet
- Give the session a minute to sync
- Check that you're signed into the same account on all devices
